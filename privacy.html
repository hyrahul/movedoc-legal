Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Movedoc is a move-in/move-out room condition documentation app. This policy explains what happens with your data.

What Movedoc collects

Nothing is sent off your device. Photos you take, room notes, and property details are stored locally on your device using local storage (SQLite). Movedoc has no server and no analytics or tracking SDKs.

Camera and Photos permission

Movedoc asks for camera access to let you photograph rooms and photo library access to attach existing photos to a report. These photos stay on your device and inside PDF reports you generate and export yourself.

In-app purchases

The one-time "unlock" purchase is processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Play Store). Movedoc does not receive or store your payment details — only a confirmation that the purchase succeeded.

Data sharing

Movedoc does not share, sell, or transmit your data to any third party, because it does not transmit data anywhere.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data. There is no account or server-side copy to delete separately.
